The idea

A different kind
of weekend.

Terra is a working countryside — real fields, real food, and real people who've spent their lives in the soil. You come for a day. You leave with dirt under your nails and a phone you forgot to check.

  • You'll pull a carrot out of the ground with your own two hands.
  • You'll eat lunch you helped cook, at a long table, outside.
  • You'll make something badly out of clay, and love it anyway.
  • You'll leave smelling like tomato leaves and woodsmoke.

A typical day

Twenty-four hours is too long. Try one.

9:00

Arrive

Boots on. Coffee in hand. The city already feels far away.

10:00

Get your hands dirty

Into the field. Weeding, picking, planting — whatever the season needs today.

12:30

Eat what the land gave you

Lunch, cooked outside, from what you just pulled out of the ground.

14:00

Make something

Clay, wood, dough — hands busy, mind quiet.

16:00

Play, or wander

Old games in the grass, or a walk with no destination. Your call.

17:30

Sit. Talk. Watch the sunset.

Nowhere to be. Nothing left to do but watch the sky go orange.
